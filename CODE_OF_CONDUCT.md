# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Nyx contributors and maintainers are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Offer constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Protect other people's privacy; do not share credentials, private data, or session contents.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Deliberate disruption of discussions or abusive conduct toward project participants is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
